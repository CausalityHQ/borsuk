## Decision

Only exact semantic parity plus a measured end-to-end latency/QPS/RAM tradeoff admits the blocked scorer for this workload. Preregistered runtime admission: in BOTH order-balanced comparisons (B1 versus A1 and B2 versus A2), candidate p90 and p95 must be at least5% lower and sequential QPS at least5% higher; all runs must satisfy the unchanged resource envelope with swap/OOM0.
