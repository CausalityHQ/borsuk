## Anchor seal and scalar neighbors

Select lowest distinct physical-unit SHA256 digests of ASCII bytes `borsuk-source-neighborhood-unit-v1` followed by one NUL byte, then the 32 binary generation-SHA bytes, then u64LE unit; compare the 32 digest bytes lexicographically, tie unit ID. Hash ASCII `borsuk-source-neighborhood-row-v1` plus NUL, the same generation bytes and u64LE unit separately; interpret its first eight digest bytes as u64LE, modulo actual final-unit row count, to derive row offset. Both literal domains and method version go into the durable seal. Authenticate canonical and collect chosen anchor ID/coords/norm and the <=32 actual rows of each selected unit during that first stream; anchors cannot be selected from labels or neighborhoods. Ascending-coordinate f64 dot and squared norms; similarity=dot/(sqrt(anchor_norm2)*sqrt(row_norm2)), clamp [-1,1], distance=1-similarity; canonicalize zero to +0. ## Evidence and interpretation

For each anchor retain exact neighbor IDs/positions/distances and counts jointly classified by own unit, own page, own leaf and destination-leaf L2 rank <=48/outside48; destination-unit counts/concentration and distinct units/leaves. Rank using existing production query normalization and frozen squared-L2 with leaf-ID ties, not a new metric. Select exactly the frozen top48 (or all available leaves in explicit small fixtures); derive selected leaf union and its physical-page closure from authenticated membership and nomination. Separately count outside selected-leaf union and outside that page closure. An unselected-leaf neighbor sharing a page with a selected unit is outside the first but inside the second. Do not label a gap bridge SOURCE closure: fetched gap bridges are unmeasured and remain separate.

Residuals are f64 ascending squared Euclidean row -> stored SQ8-derived FP16 unit mean and stored unit -> persisted unweighted leaf prototype. Do not normalize centers or reapply calibration. Also report separately named canonical selected-unit mean and within-unit dispersion: sum actual canonical row coordinates in ascending physical-row/dimension order as f64, divide by actual unit row count; then sum squared coordinate differences from that mean in the same order and divide by row count. Retain only selected-unit rows (at most anchor_count*32*D f32) to compute this direct residual without cancellation or another neighborhood search. Zero canonical means valid. These are fresh canonical statistics, distinct from the stored SQ8/FP16 means; no quantization cause is inferred from their difference.

Output always exploratory/inconclusive as to a production rewrite. The 32 anchors are the sampling units, not their correlated 1024 neighbor slots, and anchors are in-sample for this partition. Cross-leaf mass alone is insufficient. Report specifically cross-leaf AND outside-top48 AND outside physical-page closure support per anchor, alongside destination-unit concentration and canonical dispersion; unrelated within-leaf misses cannot manufacture that interaction. Unit indivisibility and intra-unit conflicting neighborhoods remain unresolved beyond the bounded descriptive statistics. That <=32 neighbors occupy <=32 destination units is tautological, not evidence that regrouping them into a64-unit leaf will work.
