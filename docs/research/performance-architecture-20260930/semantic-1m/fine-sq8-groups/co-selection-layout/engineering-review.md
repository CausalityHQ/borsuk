**The proposal supports one bounded metadata-only falsifier, but five constraints need tightening before replay.** These are design findings, not observed bugs in the unimplemented module. I reviewed both documents in full, the closed audit/reviews, and relevant Rust at `06aced808e64cb05604a16a86d6727777ac2cfc4`.

1. **P1 — Selected source vectors lack a specified authentication path.**  
   The proposal reads selected canonical vectors, but existing `Artifact` authentication provides a whole-file SHA, not independently verifiable row membership. Merely copying that SHA into an anchor receipt cannot authenticate sampled coordinates. See [anchor selection](/home/rb/worktrees/borsuk-prod-ready-v9/docs/research/performance-architecture-20260930/semantic-1m/fine-sq8-groups/co-selection-layout/research-result.md:27) and [artifact authentication](/home/rb/worktrees/borsuk-prod-ready-v9/crates/borsuk/src/hierarchical_semantic_cells.rs:69).

   **Smallest fix:** stream-hash canonical bytes while extracting the selected rows; verify their logical IDs and ordinal mapping from those same bytes. Charge the full authentication read—308 MB per 100k/D768 panel. Seal both panels’ authenticated selections, training/held split, graph/PQ identities and fitted maps before acquiring the prefix descriptor. Keep requests, GT and SQ8 bodies inaccessible.

2. **P1 — The resource declaration is not yet an executable admission bound.**  
   Eight destination blocks bound neither destination discovery nor the cost of scoring their swap partners. At 100k/C42, the straightforward sweep permits about **2.1 million candidate swaps**; scanning 4,096 anchors per swap would permit **8.6 billion anchor examinations**. Training plus held diagnostics permits **603,979,776 graph evaluations across both panels**, before fitting, PQ preparation, authentication and expansion covers. The reported 268,435,456 figure describes training for one panel only. See [resource arithmetic](/home/rb/worktrees/borsuk-prod-ready-v9/docs/research/performance-architecture-20260930/semantic-1m/fine-sq8-groups/co-selection-layout/research-result.md:117).

   **Smallest fix:** freeze explicit operation units and ceilings covering those stages, actual allocation capacities, anchor/block counts, serialization and retained buffers. Enforce them inside expensive loops. Do not inherit the old packing diagnostic’s [500-million-operation cap](/home/rb/worktrees/borsuk-prod-ready-v9/crates/borsuk/src/fine_sq8_groups.rs:1693) as qualification. Exhaustion must produce INVALID, never a partial sweep presented as the frozen algorithm.

3. **P2 — Anchor selection is described as frozen without freezing its hash bytes.**  
   “A fixed domain separator, source identity and logical ID” leaves the separator, identity definition and binary encoding unspecified. Different conforming implementations can select different 4,096 anchors. The prototype geometry also permits fewer than the required 4,608 distinct rows. See [selection rule](/home/rb/worktrees/borsuk-prod-ready-v9/docs/research/performance-architecture-20260930/semantic-1m/fine-sq8-groups/co-selection-layout/research-result.md:27).

   **Smallest fix:** name the literal domain bytes, exact source digest and integer encoding; freeze one test vector. Require exactly 4,096 training and 512 disjoint diagnostic IDs per panel, rejecting insufficient rosters. Held diagnostics must not affect fitting, dictionary selection or constants.

4. **P2 — Equal-cost cover ties can change the scored population.**  
   “Ties by object ID and offset” needs a direction and a virtual object identity. Newly constructed payload hashes are unavailable during metadata-only replay. Also, the existing helper merges the *earliest* equal smallest gap; cutting the *earliest* equal largest gap gives a different population. For selected groups `{0,2,4}` and two requests, either bridge group `1` or `3` can be fetched at identical cost. A cost-only oracle misses this difference. See [cover rule](/home/rb/worktrees/borsuk-prod-ready-v9/docs/research/performance-architecture-20260930/semantic-1m/fine-sq8-groups/co-selection-layout/research-result.md:83) and [existing merger](/home/rb/worktrees/borsuk-prod-ready-v9/crates/borsuk/src/budgeted_page_rank.rs:56).

   **Smallest fix:** specify descending gap bytes, then ascending immutable part ordinal and offset. Preserve that ordering after physical objects exist. Compare every tentative expansion’s **complete replacement cover** against the same remaining budget; tentative covers must not cumulatively consume reservations.

5. **P1 — Mandatory nominee loss is incorrectly listed as scientific REJECT.**  
   [The falsifier disposition](/home/rb/worktrees/borsuk-prod-ready-v9/docs/research/performance-architecture-20260930/semantic-1m/fine-sq8-groups/co-selection-layout/research-result.md:185) says to reject if a plan loses a nominee. With bijective placement and mandatory-group coverage, that loss indicates a mapping/planner/oracle defect. Calling it REJECT would conceal failed execution as evidence against the architecture.

   **Smallest fix:** reserve REJECT for authenticated, correctly computed envelope infeasibility. Nominee loss, oracle disagreement, corruption and resource failure are INVALID. Finish all 128 valid plans after an envelope failure; later corruption overrides REJECT. The [existing late-corruption regression](/home/rb/worktrees/borsuk-prod-ready-v9/crates/borsuk/src/fine_sq8_groups.rs:10321) already supplies the required pattern.

The **objective itself is coherent**. For its contribution `f(n)`, removal saves `2^(C+1−n)` and insertion costs `2^(C−n)`. Use checked `u128` terms and `i128` deltas. An anchor containing **both swap partners contributes zero** to the swap delta; adding two independently evaluated relaxed moves can falsely report a positive gain. Require tiny direct objective recomputation to check deltas, chosen swaps, ties, full destinations and the immovable tail.

The **cross-object optimum is also sound**: one enclosing span per required object, then omit the largest eligible internal gaps globally. The independent tiny DP/exhaustive checks should cover global request allocation, `J>K`, zero budget, short tails, equal-gap populations, already-bridged expansion, and an unaffordable candidate followed by an affordable one. Adding groups cannot repair an infeasible mandatory cover.

The scope caveats are explicit: approximately 59 GB resident admission per generation, 4N worker marks, insufficient fixed-anchor coverage, and missing durable insertion/maintenance remain unqualified. At 100M/D768 this partitioning also creates **1,163 objects**, making the mandatory-object lower bound materially different from the two-object 100k prototype. Survival establishes locality only; actual-population SQ8 ranking and separately sealed quality results remain necessary.
